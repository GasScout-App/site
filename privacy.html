GasScout Privacy Policy

Effective: November 23, 2026

GasScout ("we," "us," "our") is a gas price finder app. This policy explains what information GasScout collects, how it's used, and your choices.

Information We Collect

  • Account information: your email address and password if you sign up directly, or your name and email address if you sign in with Google or Apple.
  • Location data: while you're using the app, we access your device's location to show nearby gas stations and calculate distances to them. This is used only on your device to sort and filter results — we do not store a history of your location on our servers.
  • Fill-up reports: when you log a fill-up (price paid, gallons, fuel grade, and station), we store that information tied to your account so we can calculate and display your personal fuel savings over time.
  • Price reports: when you share a gas price, or log a fill-up, the price per gallon, fuel grade, station and time are shown on the map to everyone using GasScout. You don't need an account to do this. If you're signed in, the report is linked to your account; if you're not, it is stored with the same random install ID used for price checks, so that reports from one phone can be removed if they turn out to be false. Other users see only the price and how long ago it was reported, never who sent it or the ID. A fill-up logged without an account is not kept anywhere except as that price; sign in if you want to track your savings.
  • Price checks: when you're at a station, the app may ask whether its price is still right. If you answer, we store the station, the price you were asked about, your yes/no answer, the time, and a random ID created when the app was installed. This ID is not linked to your account, your name or your email; it only stops one phone answering about the same station over and over. Answering needs no account. Other users see only the latest answer for a station (for example, "Confirmed by a driver 2h ago"), never the ID.
  • Notifications: if you turn on notifications, we store the push address your phone gives us (an Expo push token), your usual fuel grade, and any price alert you set, together with the random install ID. We use them only to send you the weekly cheapest-gas notification and price alerts, which you can turn off any time in Settings or in your phone's settings. No account is needed and none of this is linked to your name or email.
  • App usage: each time the app is opened, we record the random install ID, whether it's iPhone or Android, the app version, whether you're signed in, and whether this install has ever shared a price. If you answer a one-tap question in the app (for example, whether you'd share prices more often with a photo), we store your answer the same way. We use this only to count how many people use GasScout and how often, and to decide what to build next. It is not linked to your account, your name, your email or your location, and it is never sold or used for advertising.

Information We Don't Collect

  • We do not use third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs.
  • We do not sell your personal data or share it with third parties for marketing purposes.

How We Use Your Information

  • To show you nearby gas stations and their current prices
  • To calculate and display your personal fuel savings based on the fill-ups you log
  • To authenticate your account and keep your data secure

Third-Party Services

  • Supabase provides authentication and data storage for the app.
  • Google Places is used to source gas station locations and fuel prices. This station/price data is public and aggregated — it is not personal data about you.
  • If you sign in with Google or Apple, those providers handle that authentication under their own privacy policies.

Data Retention & Deletion

You can permanently delete your account and all associated fill-up data at any time from Settings in the app (the gear icon at the top right) by tapping "Delete account." This action cannot be undone.

Children's Privacy

GasScout is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
